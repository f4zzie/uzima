Q: Where is the database schema?
A: src/main/resources/database_schema.sql

Q: Where is the backend API?
A: src/main/java/com/uzima/services/

Q: Where are the UI components?
A: src/main/java/com/uzima/ui/

Q: How do I run the wizard?
A: After login, click "Request Service" in MainMenuPanel

Q: Are all calculations done?
A: Yes, InvoiceService.java has all calculation methods

Q: Is data saved to database?
A: Yes, InvoiceService.createInvoice() saves to MySQL

A: Yes, update database_schema.sql and ServiceDAO.java
