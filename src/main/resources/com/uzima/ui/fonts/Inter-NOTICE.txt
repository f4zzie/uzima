Inter-*.ttf are the unmodified static fonts from the Inter 4.1 release.

InterNum-*.ttf ("Inter Num") are Modified Versions under the OFL: the digits 0-9 are mapped to
Inter's own tabular-figure glyphs (zero.tf ... nine.tf) and the family is renamed. They are built by
tools/fonts/freeze_tnum.py and are distributed under the same licence.
